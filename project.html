Getting Ready for Your Project Kickoff

Prepare for a Strong First Meeting

A successful kickoff gives the student team and partner a shared understanding of the work before research and active execution begin. Use the meeting to listen, clarify questions, and confirm how everyone will work together.

Kickoff Priorities

  • Set the agenda: Co-create a meeting plan that gives the partner and student team time to share goals and questions.
  • Clarify project bounds: Confirm the intended users, expected deliverables, timeline, and limits of the work.
  • Define communication: Agree on meeting cadence, communication channels, and response expectations.
  • Record decisions: Summarize responsibilities and next steps so all stakeholders leave with the same understanding.

What to Bring to the Kickoff

  • A draft initial client email and a clear introduction to the student team.
  • An initial client meeting agenda with questions about goals, stakeholders, and constraints.
  • A preliminary project scope that can be revised with the partner.
  • A plan for documenting the partnership agreement and next steps.

After the Meeting

Send a concise follow-up that records what the team heard, what was decided, and what still needs clarification. This creates a professional starting point and gives the partner an opportunity to correct misunderstandings before the project moves forward.
